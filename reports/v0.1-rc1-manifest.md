## What v0.1-rc1 establishes

A machine-validatable PKAF package with:

- **Stable assertion model.** RelationshipAssertion shape validates against 86 distinct assertion instances spanning local-operational, mapping, statutory authority, and registry-failure scenarios.
- **Authority chain enforcement.** AuthorityAssertionShape correctly demands `authorityKind`, `hasApplicability`, and authority-or-lifecycle evidence at A3.
- **LocalAdoption-is-not-legal-authority invariant.** Structurally enforced by `adoptionAuthorityKind` enum restriction; behaviorally validated by the statutory fixture's rescission cascade.
- **Concept resolution distinct from authority.** ConceptRegistry v0.1.2 §1.1 principle holds across all four fixtures.
- **Bridge contract executability.** BridgeValidationResult validates with required version declarations, structured remediation OR explicit no-remediation reason, and result enums.
- **Cascade closure with adoption inverse edges.** Demonstrated in the statutory fixture's `LocalAdoptionOrphanedWarning` path.

## What v0.1-rc1 does NOT cover (deferred per editorial discipline)

- ConceptRegistry shapes (Batch 2 — next work item)
- Lifecycle packet shapes (Batch 3)
- Generated artifact justification shapes (Batch 4)
- PROV-O alignment shapes
- SHACL-Core compatibility profile
- Cross-org SaaS sync semantics
- Search and Studio profiles

These are explicit non-goals for v0.1, documented in PKAF Core §10.
